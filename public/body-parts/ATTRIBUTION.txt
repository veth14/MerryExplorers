Body-part picture assets

Illustrations: original body-part flashcard artwork by Rob / Little Learning Kit.
Source: https://littlelearningkit.com/us/picture-cards/body-parts/
The source page describes the resource as free to use at home and in the classroom.

The hand, foot, eye, ear, nose, and mouth SVGs are cropped from the page's
public picture-card preview. The printed word labels were removed so the game
choices show pictures only.

Game answer cards use the corresponding local PNG illustrations in this folder.
